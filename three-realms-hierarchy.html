📜 Summary of the Three Realms Order 📜

Lowest Level → Earth Gods · Mountain Gods · City Gods · Underworld Messengers
Above Them → Star Mansions · Dragon Kings · Thunder Ministry · Yama Kings
Higher Still → Four Heavenly Kings · Nezha · Erlang Shen · Four Heavenly Masters · Four Saints · Five Elders
Sovereign → Jade Emperor (Chief of the Four Sovereigns)
Apex of Power → All Four Sovereigns
Supreme Dao → The Three Pure Ones (Embodiments of the Dao)

Parallel Path: Buddhist Attainment Line (Buddha → Bodhisattva → Arhat → Protector)
Parallel Path: Transcendent Sages (Patriarch Bodhi · Great Immortal Zhenyuan · Old Mother of Li Mountain — outside the system, peers of the Three Pure Ones & Four Sovereigns)
Demons have no rank, only outcomes — absorbed into the system or annihilated.
