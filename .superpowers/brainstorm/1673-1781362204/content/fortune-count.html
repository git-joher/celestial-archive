签文规模 — How many fortunes?

Traditional temples have 100 lots. Each one needs: a 4-line poem, interpretation, deity message, life domain guidance, and English translation. That's a lot of content.

A

28 Lots (二十八宿)

One for each Lunar Mansion. 7 per tier (上上×5, 上×8, 中×8, 下×5, 下下×2). Manageable content scope, rich astronomical theme. Each fortune tied to a constellation.

B

36 Lots (三十六天罡)

One for each Heavenly Spirit star. 36 fortunes with even distribution. Good balance of variety and content effort. Tied to the 36 Stars of Destiny from Water Margin / Daoist cosmology.

C

60 Lots (六十甲子)

One for each sexagenary cycle combination. 60 fortunes — the traditional middle ground used by many temples. Rich 干支 theme. More content but thematically perfect.

D

100 Lots (百签全典)

Full traditional set. 100 fortunes. The most authentic but enormous content undertaking. Could start with a subset and expand over time.

Also: How do we generate the fortune content?

Classical Chinese poems + deity messages + interpretations is specialized content.

X

LLM-generated + human review

Generate fortune poems and interpretations via LLM, then review and curate. Fast, consistent quality, easy to produce at scale.

Y

Mixed: reference real classics + adapt

Base poems on real classical Chinese poetry and 签文 from actual temples (public domain), adapt and remix with deity-specific twists.

Z

Hand-write with LLM help

Design the structure and themes, write key poems by hand, use LLM to fill in the rest with careful curation.
