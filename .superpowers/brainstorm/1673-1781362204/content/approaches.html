天庭求签 — Which interaction style feels right?

The core question: how does the user "draw a lot" from the celestial court? Each approach creates a different emotional experience.

Approach A: Temple Shake (摇签筒)

Shake a virtual bamboo cylinder — sticks rattle until one falls out. Very tactile, closest to real temple divination. Mobile-first: uses device gyroscope/accelerometer, falls back to click-to-shake on desktop.

Approach B: Deity Oracle Circle (神谕轮盘)

A celestial wheel/circle with deity symbols spins and lands on your fortune. Visually dramatic, gives a "wheel of fate" feeling. The selected deity determines the flavor of the fortune.

Approach C: Sacred Lot Pool (圣签池)

A pool of floating sacred lots (numbered sticks). User reaches in and picks one — like drawing a card from a mystical pond. More contemplative, less game-like. Each lot glows with different intensity.

A

Temple Shake 🎋

Gyroscope-powered bamboo cylinder. Tactile & authentic. Best on mobile. Falls back to click-shake on desktop.

B

Deity Oracle Wheel 🎡

Spinning celestial wheel. Visually spectacular. Each deity gives themed fortunes. Works equally on all devices.

C

Sacred Lot Pool 🌊

Mystical floating sticks. Contemplative mood. Pick one from the cosmic pond. More meditative, less game-like.

D

Hybrid: Shake → Wheel → Reveal

Combine A+B: shake to "awaken the oracle", wheel selects the deity, then reveal the fortune. Multi-phase ritual.
