签文结构 — What does a fortune slip look like?

Each fortune needs a structure. Which combination feels right for "天庭求签"?

A real Chinese temple fortune slip (签文) contains:

第 四十二 签

上上签 · 大吉

云开日出见光明
花发春来万物荣
行人已至家书近
自有贵人指路行

解曰：时机将至，贵人相助。宜守不宜攻，静待佳音。所求之事，三月内必有结果。

Question 1: Fortune tier system (签等)

Traditional temples grade lots from best to worst. Should we include tiers?

A

Full 5-tier (traditional)

上上签 · 上签 · 中签 · 下签 · 下下签 — full authentic temple grading with proportionally distributed odds

B

Positive-only (3 tiers)

大吉 · 吉 · 平 — skip the bad fortunes. Keep the experience uplifting. Nobody wants a "下下签" as a web experience.

C

No tiers — pure wisdom

All fortunes are poetic wisdom. No grading at all — each is a unique message from the deity. More like an oracle than a lottery.

Question 2: Fortune content structure

What should each fortune contain?

1

签诗 (Fortune Poem)

4-line classical Chinese poem — the heart of traditional 签文. Essential for authenticity.

2

解签 (Interpretation)

Plain-language explanation of what the poem means for your life. Bridges classical poetry to modern understanding.

3

Deity Personal Message

A personal note from the deity in their unique voice (like inscriptions.js). Sun Wukong's would be bold, Guanyin's compassionate, etc.

4

Life Domain Guidance

Specific guidance for: Career · Love · Health · Wealth · Travel. Each fortune maps to one domain.

5

English Translation

Full English rendering of the poem and interpretation. Important for an English-language website — not all visitors read Chinese.
