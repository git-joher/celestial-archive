神兽收集 — Scale & Regions

How many beasts, across how many regions?

Q1: Collection Size

A

20 Beasts · 小型图鉴

4 regions × 5 beasts each. Tight curation, highest quality per beast. Faster to build. Each region feels distinct.

B

30 Beasts · 中型图鉴

6 regions × 5 beasts each. Good breadth. Covers the major categories (dragons, birds, beasts, spirits, sea creatures, underworld). Sweet spot.

C

50 Beasts · 大型图鉴

7 regions × ~7 beasts each. Encyclopedia-level. Most impressive but significant content work. Future expansions possible.

Q2: Map Regions

Chinese mythology divides the world into sacred mountains, seas, and realms. Which regions?

1

昆仑仙境 · Kunlun Paradise

Home of the Queen Mother of the West. Qilin, White Tiger, celestial deer, immortal cranes.

2

东海龙宫 · Eastern Sea

Dragon kings' underwater realm. Azure Dragon, dragon turtles, krakens, pearl-producing mermaids.

3

南山经 · Southern Mountains

Verdant jungles from the Classic of Mountains and Seas. Vermilion Bird, nine-tailed fox, xingxing apes.

4

西荒大漠 · Western Wastes

Deserts and barren lands. Qiongqi, taotie, giant scorpions, sand wyrms.

5

北冥冰原 · Northern Ice Plains

Frozen seas and dark forests. Black Tortoise, kun-peng, ice phoenix, snow serpents.

6

天庭神兽 · Celestial Realm

Heaven's divine menagerie. Dragon-horses, golden crows, moon rabbits, heavenly dogs.

7

幽冥地府 · Underworld

The dark realm beneath. Huli jing, ghost serpents, soul-guiding birds, ox-head horse-face guardians.
