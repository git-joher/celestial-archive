神兽卡片 — What does a beast card contain?

Each collected beast gets a card in the bestiary. What information should it display?

Choose which elements to include on each beast card:

1

名称 (Name)

Chinese name + English name. Essential.

2

神兽图鉴 (Illustration)

A visual representation — SVG icon, emoji, or image placeholder. Gives each beast visual identity.

3

古籍原文 (Classic Source Text)

The original Classical Chinese description from 山海经 or other ancient texts. Scholarly authenticity.

4

传说故事 (Lore & Stories)

A paragraph (zh+en) about the beast's mythology — famous appearances, legends, cultural significance.

5

属性数值 (Stats)

Game-like attributes: Power, Wisdom, Mystery, Ferocity, Benevolence. Fun for comparison and collection.

6

栖息地 (Habitat & Region)

Where the beast lives — region on the map, terrain type. Connects to the exploration mechanic.

7

稀有度 (Rarity Tier)

Common / Rare / Legendary / Mythic. Drives collection satisfaction and encounter rates.

8

分类 (Beast Type)

Dragon / Phoenix / Qilin / Beast / Spirit / Demon. Categorization for filtering the bestiary.
