神兽收集 — Rarity & Encounter Mechanics

How rare is each beast, and what happens when you explore a region?

Q1: Rarity Tiers & Distribution (20 beasts)

A

3 Tiers: 普通 · 稀有 · 传说

Common (10) / Rare (6) / Legendary (4). Simple and clear. Each region has 1 legendary beast as its "boss."

B

4 Tiers: 凡 · 灵 · 仙 · 神

Mortal (8) / Spirit (6) / Immortal (4) / Divine (2). Thematically Chinese tiers. Divine beasts are almost mythical in rarity — only 2 exist in the entire collection.

Q2: Exploration Mechanic

When the user clicks a region to explore, what happens?

A

Automatic Encounter

Click region → short narrative text plays out ("You venture into the misty peaks of Kunlun...") → a beast is randomly revealed from that region's pool (weighted by rarity). Simple, no friction. One free exploration per day.

B

Riddle Challenge

Click region → a classical riddle or clue about a specific beast appears → user guesses which beast it is from 3 options → correct guess reveals the beast. Wrong guess still reveals it but with a "near miss" note. More engaging.

C

Footprint Tracking

Click region → see "tracks" or "signs" of 2-3 beasts → choose which trail to follow → encounter the chosen beast. Gives user agency over which beast they pursue. Rare beasts have fainter tracks.
