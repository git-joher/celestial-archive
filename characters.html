Chinese Mythology Characters

Legendary heroes, immortal pilgrims, demon kings, and the unforgettable figures who walk between heaven and earth

Chinese mythology is populated by characters of extraordinary depth — rebels who storm heaven, monks who walk through hell, dragon princes who serve in silence, and demon kings who rule vast territories. The most famous of them all is Sun Wukong, the Monkey King, whose rebellion against the celestial order is the defining arc of Journey to the West. Alongside him stand Zhu Bajie, the gluttonous but powerful fallen marshal; Sha Wujing, the silent steadfast guardian; Tang Sanzang, the pilgrim monk who binds them all; and the White Dragon Horse, the prince who carried the journey on his back. But every journey begins with a teacher — Patriarch Subodhi, the Hidden Sage who taught Sun Wukong everything, then vanished from the story entirely. Beyond the pilgrims, characters like Bull Demon King — Sun Wukong's sworn brother turned adversary — and the warrior god Nezha bring conflict, loyalty, and tragedy to the mythological stage. Each of these Chinese myth characters embodies a facet of the human condition: pride, devotion, rebellion, and redemption.
