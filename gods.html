Chinese Gods & Deities

The divine beings who rule heaven, earth, and the underworld — from the supreme Jade Emperor to the compassionate Guanyin

The pantheon of Chinese gods is one of the richest and most complex in human history. Unlike Western mythologies with a single creation story and fixed hierarchy, Chinese divinity evolved over millennia through the blending of Taoist, Buddhist, and folk traditions. The Jade Emperor presides over a celestial bureaucracy modeled on imperial China — gods hold offices, file reports, and can be promoted or demoted. Below the heavenly court, bodhisattvas like Guanyin extend compassion across realms, while warrior gods like Nezha and Erlang Shen defend cosmic order. And then there are the boundary-crossers — beings like Sun Wukong, the Monkey King, who defy every category and every heaven. Whether you are looking for the most powerful Chinese gods, the most worshipped, or the most legendary, this page is your gateway to every major deity in the Celestial Archive.
