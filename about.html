Our Mission

Celestial Archive is dedicated to exploring and sharing the rich mythology of Eastern cultures — from the stone-born Monkey King to the celestial halls of the Jade Emperor, from the eight immortals of Daoist legend to the thousand-handed Guanyin. These stories shaped civilizations, inspired art across centuries, and remain startlingly alive today in film, games, and literature worldwide.

We believe these myths deserve an archive as immersive as the stories themselves. Every page is crafted to honor the aesthetic of classical Chinese art — ink-wash painting, Dunhuang mural warmth, and the quiet elegance of ancient manuscripts.

What We Cover

Our content spans the full breadth of Eastern mythology: legendary heroes and divine beings, epic journeys and cosmic rebellions, sacred weapons and transformative powers. We trace each figure from their origins in classical texts through their cultural legacy in modern media — opera, television, film, anime, manga, and video games.

We launched with Sun Wukong, the Monkey King — one of the most beloved and globally influential figures in all of mythology. More deities and legendary figures will join the archive over time.

Who We Are

Celestial Archive is an independent passion project — conceived, designed, and built by a solo creator with the assistance of AI tools for research, writing, and image generation. Every page reflects a personal fascination with Eastern mythology and a belief that these ancient stories deserve a beautiful home on the modern web. We are not affiliated with any religious or political organization, and we welcome corrections and contributions from the community.
