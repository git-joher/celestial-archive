Privacy Policy

How we handle your data and respect your privacy.

Last updated: May 9, 2026

1. Who We Are

Celestial Archive is a static website exploring Eastern mythology. We do not operate a backend server, collect personal data, or require user accounts. The site is served as static HTML, CSS, and JavaScript files.

2. Information We Collect

We do not collect, store, or process any personal information. Celestial Archive has no forms that transmit data to any server, no user registration, and no backend database. The only data stored is:

  • Local messages (Golden Inscriptions): Messages you write on our Sun Wukong page are stored exclusively in your browser's localStorage. This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us or any third party. You can clear it at any time by clearing your browser data.

3. Cookies and Tracking

Celestial Archive itself does not set any cookies and does not use any analytics or tracking scripts.

Third-party advertising partners may use cookies and similar technologies to serve personalized ads. These partners include but are not limited to Google AdSense. These third parties may:

  • Use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites
  • Collect anonymous browsing data for ad personalization and measurement
  • Use web beacons and similar technologies for ad delivery and reporting

You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting:

  • Google Ads Settings
  • Digital Advertising Alliance Opt-Out
  • European Interactive Digital Advertising Alliance

4. External Links

Our pages may contain links to external websites (e.g., official game websites, Wikipedia, streaming platforms). We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of these third-party sites.

5. Children's Privacy

Celestial Archive is a general-audience educational website about mythology. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through a third-party service accessed via our site, please contact us.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.

7. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please visit our Contact page.
