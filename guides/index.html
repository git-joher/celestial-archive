Chinese Mythology Guides

From beginner introductions to expert deep dives — your starting point for understanding Chinese myth, gods, and legends

Chinese mythology can feel overwhelming at first. With thousands of years of history, a pantheon of hundreds of Chinese gods, and a sprawling epic like Journey to the West weaving through it all, knowing where to start is half the battle. That is why we created these Chinese mythology guides — curated pathways through the tradition, organized by difficulty and topic. For absolute beginners, our introductory guides explain the basic cosmology: the structure of heaven, the role of the Jade Emperor, and the founding myths of Pangu and Nüwa. For those who want character deep dives, we have dedicated guides to the most popular figures: Sun Wukong, Nezha, the nature of Nezha's divinity, and the complete cast of Journey to the West. And for enthusiasts ready for comparisons and deep dives, we offer side-by-side analyses like Nezha vs Sun Wukong, Sun Wukong vs Erlang Shen, and Chinese vs Greek Mythology. Every guide is written for the curious reader — rigorous but accessible, detailed but inviting.

Frequently Asked Questions About Chinese Mythology Guides

Q: Where should a beginner start with Chinese mythology?

Start with the creation myths — Pangu Creates the World and Nüwa Repairs the Sky — which establish the Chinese cosmological framework in just a few paragraphs. Then read our Chinese Mythology 101 guide for an overview of heaven, the underworld, and the celestial bureaucracy. From there, dive into character stories: Nezha is a great second stop because his story is self-contained and dramatic. Finally, begin the Journey to the West cycle, which ties everything together. This path takes you from zero knowledge to a solid understanding of Chinese mythology in the most natural progression.

Q: What is the best book to start learning Chinese mythology?

For English readers, the best starting point is Journey to the West translated by W.J.F. Jenner or Anthony Yu — the novel is the single richest source of Chinese mythological characters and stories. For a reference guide, The Handbook of Chinese Mythology by Lihui Yang and Deming An is the standard academic introduction. For a more narrative approach, Chinese Myths and Legends by Lianshan Chen covers the major myths in an accessible style. All of these complement the free Chinese mythology guides available here on Celestial Archive.
